# Commercial Beta Gate

## Founding offer

**DeFi Market Risk Report — Founding Beta: €9**

The purpose of the first paid test is not to maximize revenue. It is to validate whether a buyer will pay for evidence-backed DeFi market risk intelligence.

## Included

- verified live market snapshot;
- APY, utilization and available liquidity;
- six-dimension risk score;
- factor-level explanations;
- provenance and evidence registry;
- methodology confidence;
- explicit data-quality and commercial gates;
- beta-methodology disclosure.

## Not included

- personalized allocation advice;
- wallet-specific suitability;
- custody;
- transaction execution;
- auto-rebalancing;
- guarantees or predictions of loss.

## Validation gate

After the first 10 qualified prospects:

- if at least 2 pay or explicitly request a paid follow-up, continue and test €19;
- if buyers value alerts more than static reports, prioritize monitoring;
- if nobody will pay, investigate the objection before adding features.

The report is a low-cost validation product. The scoring methodology remains beta until it has been benchmarked across multiple protocols, assets and historical stress periods.
