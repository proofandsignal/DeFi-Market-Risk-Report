# DeFi Market Risk Report — Founding Beta

Most DeFi lending dashboards answer:

> What is the APY?

Proof & Signal asks a different question:

> What risk is sitting behind that APY right now, and what evidence supports the answer?

## What you get

For one DeFi lending market:

- current supply APY;
- utilization and available liquidity;
- Protocol Risk;
- Asset Risk;
- Liquidity Risk;
- Oracle Risk;
- Stablecoin Risk;
- Chain Risk;
- the highest-scoring risk factors;
- source provenance and timestamps;
- evidence-confidence status;
- explicit data-quality gate.

## Pilot market

**Aave V3 Ethereum Core / USDC**

In the first live report, the overall score was moderate while the liquidity dimension was materially higher because utilization was around 94%.

That distinction is the point: a headline APY alone does not show the full market condition.

## What this is not

- not personalized investment advice;
- not a recommendation to deposit or borrow;
- not custody;
- not a probability of loss;
- not a guarantee;
- not a statistically calibrated credit rating.

The current score is a transparent **beta comparative heuristic** backed by verifiable evidence.

## Why only €9?

This is a validation price.

The goal is to learn whether evidence-backed DeFi market due diligence is valuable enough to pay for before building a larger product.

Founding beta buyers are buying the report as it exists today — not a promise of future features.
