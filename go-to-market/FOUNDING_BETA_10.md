## Qualification rules

A prospect counts as **qualified** only if at least one is true:

1. They actively allocate to or research DeFi lending/yield.
2. They publish DeFi/yield research for an audience.
3. They build a lending/yield interface whose users need risk context.
4. They operate risk/yield infrastructure where methodology feedback is commercially meaningful.

A “like”, view, follow, or generic positive comment is **not** validation.

## Success gate after 10 contacts

- **Strong signal:** >=2 paid purchases, or 1 purchase + >=2 explicit requests for another paid report.
- **Promising:** no purchase yet, but >=3 prospects explicitly say they would pay for alerts, wallet monitoring, comparative reports or a specific added evidence layer.
- **Weak:** polite feedback with no willingness to pay.
- **Fail / investigate:** 10 qualified contacts, zero payment intent, zero clear paid alternative.

Do not add more features before recording why the first 10 did or did not care.
