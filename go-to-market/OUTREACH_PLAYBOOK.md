# Founding Beta Outreach Playbook

## Product

**Proof & Signal — DeFi Market Risk Report**

Founding Beta price: **€9**

Pilot report:

**Aave V3 Ethereum Core / USDC**

Current methodology output in Report #001:

- six evidence-backed risk dimensions;
- live APY / utilization / liquidity;
- factor-level risk drivers;
- source provenance;
- data freshness / confidence;
- explicit PASS / VERIFY / UNKNOWN gates;
- beta methodology disclosure.

The report is decision support, not personalized investment advice.

## Default X / Telegram DM

Hi — I’m testing a small DeFi research product called **Proof & Signal Market Risk Report**.

Instead of showing only APY, it breaks one lending market into protocol, asset, liquidity, oracle, stablecoin and chain risk, with the evidence behind every score.

The first live report is **Aave Ethereum / USDC**. It flagged liquidity as the main pressure point because utilization was ~94%, even though the overall market score remained moderate.

I’m deliberately charging **€9 for the founding beta** because I want to learn whether this is actually worth paying for, not collect polite feedback.

Would you be open to testing one report?

## Researcher-specific DM

I built an evidence-backed **DeFi Market Risk Report** that puts a risk layer beside APY: liquidity, utilization, caps, oracle, asset/stablecoin controls, chain risk, provenance and confidence.

If yes, I’ll send the exact pilot details. If not, a one-line “why not” is genuinely useful.

## Product-team / strategic validation message

Hi — I’m building **Proof & Signal DeFi Market Risk Report**, an independent evidence-backed risk layer for lending markets.

The current pilot covers Aave Ethereum / USDC and the engine separates protocol, asset, liquidity, oracle, stablecoin and chain risk, with data-quality and evidence-confidence gates.

I noticed your product/research already deals with lending or yield risk, so I’m not pitching this as “another APY dashboard.” I’d value a critical look at the methodology and whether an external market-risk layer like this could be useful to your users or research workflow.

The consumer founding beta is priced at €9; for you I’m primarily testing the B2B/design-partner signal.

## Follow-up after 3–5 days

Quick follow-up on the DeFi Market Risk Report.

I’m keeping the test intentionally small: 10 qualified people, €9 founding beta, and I stop adding features until I understand the objections.

If a static report isn’t useful, which would be more valuable to you?

- cross-protocol comparison;
- liquidation / scenario simulation;
- live alerts when risk changes;
- wallet/position monitoring;
- API / structured risk data;
- none of these.

A one-line answer is enough.

## After purchase

Thanks — this is a founding beta, so the most useful feedback is not “looks good.”

After reading it, please answer:

1. What did the report tell you that APY/TVL alone did not?
2. Which section did you ignore?
3. Was any score/explanation hard to trust?
4. Would you pay €19 for the next report?
5. Would you rather pay for one-off reports, alerts/monitoring, or an API?

## Rules

- Do not say the model predicts losses.
- Do not call the report a credit rating.
- Do not tell a prospect what to buy, sell, supply, borrow or allocate.
- Do not claim a prospect used, bought, reviewed or endorsed the product until that actually happened.
- Do not offer discounts before testing the €9 price.
- Do not send mass outreach. The first 10 should be individually relevant.
